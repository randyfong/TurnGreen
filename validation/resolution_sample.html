Formal Policy Clauses
  • WHEREAS, urban heat islands and severe precipitation events directly impact residents, youth, and neighborhood infrastructure within District 6 - Central Urban Basin;
  • WHEREAS, empirical biophysical modeling through Stanford University's InVEST urban cooling engine demonstrates that expanding vegetative canopy and cool surfaces can lower local summer surface temperatures by -0.9°C (-1.6°F);
  • WHEREAS, impervious surface coverage (ESA WorldCover Class 50) exacerbates street-level runoff, and bio-retention interventions modeled by USDA-SCS Curve Number hydrology divert up to 4,850 m³ of runoff during a severe design storm;
  • WHEREAS, the 5-year simulation scenario completed by Maya Lin (Lincoln High Youth Environmental Action) models verified land cover transitions aligning with UN SDG 15.3.1 Land Degradation Neutrality targets within a $200,000 municipal budget.
  • NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the City Council shall formally adopt a natural infrastructure target to de-pave a minimum of 9.8 hectares of impervious ground and retrofit 5.0 hectares of rooftop area across District 6 - Central Urban Basin;
  • NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, municipal capital improvement authorities shall prioritize nature-based solutions, including shade canopy corridors and roadside bioswales, in future roadway and sidewalk projects;
  • NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the City Council establishes a permanent Youth Climate Advisory Seat to review annual infrastructure investments and track local progress toward climate resilience.
